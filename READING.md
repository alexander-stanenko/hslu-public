# Further reading

The reading list from the last slides of Module 3, easiest first. Links checked on 14 November 2026.

## Start here

- Anthropic, [Building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents) (2024). Workflows and agents, and the workflow patterns of the afternoon.
- OpenAI, [A practical guide to building agents](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-agents.pdf) (2025). The same split from the other vendor, with guardrails.
- Anthropic, [Writing effective tools for agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/writing-tools-for-agents) (2025). The tool guidelines of Lab 1, Part 3.
- Model Context Protocol, [Introduction](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/docs/getting-started/intro). Hosts, clients and servers.
- Simon Willison, [The lethal trifecta for AI agents](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Jun/16/the-lethal-trifecta/) (2025). Why the tyre shop's notice in Lab 2 is a risk.

## Papers we cited

- Yao et al., [ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629) (ICLR 2023). The agent loop.
- Sumers et al., [Cognitive Architectures for Language Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.02427) (TMLR 2024). The kinds of memory.
- Anthropic, [How we built our multi-agent research system](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system) (2025).
- Cemri et al., [Why Do Multi-Agent LLM Systems Fail?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13657) (2025).
- Kim et al., [Towards a Science of Scaling Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08296) (2026).

## Meaning and ontologies

- Jessica Talisman, [The Ontology Pipeline](https://jessicatalisman.substack.com/p/the-ontology-pipeline) (2025). From a controlled vocabulary to a knowledge graph, step by step.
- Philipp Warmer and Grant Kalil, Building an AI-Native Data Platform (D ONE point of view, October 2026), [d-one.ai](https://d-one.ai). The staircase from data platform to knowledge graph. D ONE is the lecturer's employer.
- Sequeda, Allemang and Jacob, [A Benchmark to Understand the Role of Knowledge Graphs on Large Language Model's Accuracy for Question Answering on Enterprise SQL Databases](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07509) (2023).
- Databricks, [Announcing the general availability of Genie Ontology](https://www.databricks.com/blog/announcing-ga-genie-ontology) (11 November 2026). One platform's version of the layer; its benchmark is the vendor's own.
