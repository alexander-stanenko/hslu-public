# Lakeside Insurance AG — General Terms and Conditions: Motor Vehicle Insurance (Edition 2025)

_Fictional document created for teaching purposes._

## §1 Scope
These terms apply to motor vehicle insurance contracts of Lakeside Insurance AG ("Lakeside"). The insured vehicle, the modules chosen and the deductibles are stated in the policy schedule. Cover applies in Switzerland and in Europe.

## §2 Liability (mandatory)
Every motor policy includes third-party liability insurance as required by Swiss law. It covers personal injury and property damage that the driver causes to third parties. **Damage to the insured vehicle itself is not covered by liability insurance.** Own-vehicle damage requires partial cover (§3) and/or collision cover (§4).

## §3 Partial cover (Teilkasko)
If partial cover is included in the policy schedule, Lakeside covers damage to the insured vehicle caused by:
- **Glass breakage**: windscreen, side and rear windows, glass roofs (§6.2 glass deductible applies).
- **Natural hazards**: hail, storm (wind of at least 75 km/h), flooding, rockfall, avalanche, snow pressure.
- **Theft** of the vehicle or of parts of it, including attempted theft.
- **Fire**, explosion and short circuit.
- **Collision with animals** (e.g. deer, fox, wild boar) on public roads.
- **Vandalism** (malicious damage by third parties).

## §4 Collision cover (Vollkasko)
If collision cover is included, Lakeside additionally covers damage to the insured vehicle caused by a collision for which the driver is responsible, e.g. driving into a pillar, wall, bollard or another vehicle. Collision cover is only available together with partial cover.

## §5 Sum insured and compensation
Repairs are compensated at the invoiced amount of a professional repair. If the vehicle is stolen and not recovered within 30 days, or is a total loss, compensation is based on the current market value. Rental cars are only covered if the optional rental-car module is included.

## §6 Deductibles
6.1 The deductible stated in the policy schedule is subtracted from every claim payment.
6.2 For **glass breakage**, the separate **glass deductible** from the policy schedule applies (CHF 0 or CHF 200). It replaces the partial-cover deductible.
6.3 For **collision** claims the collision deductible applies (standard: CHF 1,000).
6.4 For all other partial-cover claims, the partial-cover deductible applies (CHF 200, 300 or 500 according to the schedule).

## §7 Exclusions and reductions
7.1 Not covered: wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, damage from wrong fuel, damage during races.
7.2 **Gross negligence**: if the insured event was caused by gross negligence — for example leaving the vehicle unlocked with the key inside, or driving under the influence — Lakeside may reduce the payment in proportion to the degree of fault. Such cases are assessed by a claims adjuster.
7.3 Damage that existed before the start of the policy is not covered.

## §8 Obligations after a claim
8.1 The policyholder must provide all information and documents needed to assess the claim.
8.2 **Theft, attempted theft and vandalism must be reported to the police**; the police report must be submitted to Lakeside.
8.3 **Collisions with animals must be reported to the police or the game warden immediately**; the confirmation must be submitted.
8.4 For repairs, a repair invoice or a professional cost estimate must be submitted.

## §9 Reporting deadline
Claims must be reported to Lakeside **within 30 days** of the incident. Late reports are not automatically rejected, but Lakeside reviews whether the delay affected the assessment of the claim.

## §10 Fraud
If the policyholder deliberately provides false information or documents, Lakeside may refuse all payment and terminate the contract. Lakeside may consult claim histories and price lists to verify claims.
