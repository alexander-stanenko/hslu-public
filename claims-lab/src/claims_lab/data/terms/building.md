# Lakeside Insurance AG — General Terms and Conditions: Building Insurance (Edition 2025)

_Fictional document created for teaching purposes._

## §1 Scope
Building insurance covers the building stated in the policy schedule, including fixed installations (heating, plumbing, built-in kitchen), up to the sum insured.

## §2 Basic cover
The basic cover includes damage to the building caused by:
- **Fire**, lightning, explosion.
- **Natural hazards**: hail, storm (wind of at least 75 km/h), flooding, rockfall, snow pressure.
- **Water damage**: sudden escape of water from pipes and installations inside the building, including the cost of locating the leak and repairing the pipe.

## §3 Optional add-on: building glass
Breakage of window panes, glass doors and glass facades. Only covered if this add-on is included in the policy schedule.

## §4 Compensation
Repairs are compensated at the invoiced cost of professional repair up to the sum insured.

## §5 Exclusions
Not covered are:
- damage caused by **wear and tear, ageing or lack of maintenance** (e.g. brittle or porous roof tiles, rotten window frames);
- gradual seepage and condensation;
- damage that existed before the insured event.
If an insured event (e.g. a storm) and lack of maintenance both contributed to the damage, the claim is assessed by an adjuster, who determines the covered share.

## §6 Deductibles
6.1 Natural-hazard damage (hail, storm, flooding): CHF 1,000 per claim.
6.2 All other damage (fire, water, glass): CHF 500 per claim.

## §7 Emergency measures
The policyholder must take reasonable measures to limit the damage (e.g. temporary roof covering). The costs of such measures are covered.

## §8 Obligations after a claim and reporting deadline
8.1 Claims must be reported **within 30 days** of the incident; late reports are reviewed by an adjuster.
8.2 An invoice or cost estimate from a professional company must be submitted.
