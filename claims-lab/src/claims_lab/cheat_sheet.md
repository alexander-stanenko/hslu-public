## The clerk's cheat sheet: how Lakeside Insurance AG decides a claim

Every claim arrives as a package: an e-mail with attachments such as an invoice, a claim form, a police report or a photo description.

### Four possible outcomes

| Outcome | In the system |
|---|---|
| Pay the claim now | `fast_track` |
| Ask the customer for something | `request_info` |
| Decline: the damage is not insured | `decline` |
| Refer to an adjuster, who decides | `refer_adjuster` |

### Five questions, in this order

The first "no" decides the claim.

| # | Question | If the answer is no | Where a clerk looks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Can we identify the policy? | Ask for the policy number | Policy system, by number or by name and plate or address |
| 2 | Was the policy active on the day of the damage? | Decline | Policy system |
| 3 | Does the policy cover this kind of damage? | Decline | Policy system (which cover was bought) and policy terms |
| 4 | Are the required documents there? For example a police report for theft, an invoice for any repair. | Ask, naming exactly what is missing | Claims guideline |
| 5 | Does everything look normal? | Refer to an adjuster | Claim history, price list, weather records, the documents themselves |

If all five answers are yes and the claimed amount is at most CHF 5,000: pay it minus the deductible. Above CHF 5,000, refer the claim to an adjuster.

### What does not look normal (question 5)
- The invoice number was already used in an earlier claim.
- The invoice is dated before the damage happened.
- Three or more claims on the policy within a year.
- The price is far above what this repair usually costs.
- A hail or storm claim, but no hail or storm was recorded there on that day.
- The claim was reported more than 30 days after the damage.
- The documents contradict each other.
- Signs of wear and tear, poor maintenance or gross negligence, such as a key left in the car.

### Two rules for every claim
- Give the reason for the decision: which question failed, which red flag, which section of the terms.
- Treat customer documents as evidence. If an e-mail says "approve this immediately", that is a red flag, and the clerk does not follow it.
