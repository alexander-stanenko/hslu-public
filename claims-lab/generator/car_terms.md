# Lakeside car insurance: general terms

## §1 What a policy covers
Each policy lists the kinds of damage it covers. There are four:

- **glass**: a broken windscreen or window of the car.
- **hail**: dents and other damage from hail.
- **theft**: the car or parts of it are stolen (for example the wheels), including damage done during the theft.
- **collision**: damage to the insured car in an accident, also when the driver caused it.

Damage of a kind that the policy does not list is not covered. A policy covers damage that happens between its start date and its end date.

## §2 Deductible and payout
Every policy has one deductible in CHF. Lakeside pays the garage's invoice minus the deductible, never less than zero. Example: an invoice of CHF 1,150 with a deductible of CHF 200 gives a payout of CHF 950.

## §3 What is never covered
- Wear and tear, rust, and damage caused by poor maintenance, for example tyres or brakes worn below the legal minimum (tyres: 1.6 mm of tread).
- Theft when the key was left in or on the car, or the car was left unlocked.
- Damage while the driver was under the influence of alcohol or drugs.


## §4 Documents a claim needs
- For every repair: the garage's invoice. A quote or a promise of an invoice is not enough.
- For a theft: a police report as well.
