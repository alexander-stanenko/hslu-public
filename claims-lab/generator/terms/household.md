# Lakeside Insurance AG — General Terms and Conditions: Household Contents Insurance (Edition 2025)

_Fictional document created for teaching purposes._

## §1 Scope
Household contents insurance covers movable property owned by the policyholder and the persons living in the same household, at the address stated in the policy schedule. The sum insured is stated in the policy schedule.

## §2 Basic cover
The basic cover includes damage to household contents caused by:
- **Fire**, smoke, lightning, explosion.
- **Natural hazards**: hail, storm (wind of at least 75 km/h), flooding, landslide. Garden furniture and items on balconies and terraces are included.
- **Water damage**: sudden escape of water from pipes, appliances (washing machine, dishwasher, fridge) and aquariums.
- **Burglary**: theft after forced entry into the home or the cellar, and robbery.

## §3 Optional add-ons
3.1 **Theft outside the home** (simple theft): theft of items outside the home, e.g. a bicycle at the station or a phone on public transport. **Only covered if this add-on is included in the policy schedule.**
3.2 **Glass breakage**: breakage of furniture glass, glass ceramic hobs, mirrors and aquariums. Only covered if this add-on is included.

## §4 Exclusions
Not covered are:
- damage caused by **gradual** effects such as slowly leaking connections, seepage, condensation, damp or mould that develops over time;
- wear and tear;
- loss or misplacing of items;
- damage to vehicles (covered by motor insurance).

## §5 Sum insured and compensation
Damaged items are compensated at their replacement value (new value) up to the sum insured. Repairs are compensated up to the replacement value.

## §6 Deductible
A deductible of CHF 200 per claim applies unless the policy schedule states otherwise.

## §7 Proof of ownership
For theft and burglary, the policyholder must prove ownership and value of the stolen items, e.g. with purchase receipts, invoices, photographs or valuations.

## §8 Obligations after a claim and reporting deadline
8.1 Claims must be reported **within 30 days** of the incident; late reports are reviewed by an adjuster.
8.2 **Burglary and theft must be reported to the police**; the police report must be submitted together with a list of the stolen items and proof of ownership (§7).
8.3 For water and natural-hazard damage, an invoice or cost estimate of a professional company must be submitted.
